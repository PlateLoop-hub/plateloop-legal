Delete your PlateLoop account

PlateLoop (Android app) · Last updated: 14 November 2026

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open PlateLoop and sign in.
  2. Clients: tap the gear icon on Today to open Settings. Trainers and nutritionists: open the Account tab.
  3. Tap Request account deletion and confirm. You are signed out straight away.

Option 2: by email

If you no longer have the app, email support.plateloop@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my PlateLoop account”. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address. Never send your password.

What is deleted

Within 30 days of your request we delete:

  • your account, name, email address and profile details;
  • booking requests, including goals and health notes you wrote;
  • messages you sent, your reviews, reports and blocks;
  • for professionals: your public profile, services, documents and payout account details.

What is kept

If you made or received payments through PlateLoop, the payment records (amounts, dates, transaction IDs, receipt images and payout records) are kept without your name or contact details, for as long as accounting and tax law requires. Messages the other person sent you stay in their own booking history.

Data on your phone

Your meal, water, activity and sleep logs are stored only on your phone and never on our servers. To remove them, use Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the app.
