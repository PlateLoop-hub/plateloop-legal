PlateLoop Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026 · Applies to the PlateLoop Android app

Summary

  • Your tracking data (meals, water, activity, sleep, body details, reminders) is stored only on your phone. It is never uploaded.
  • If you create an account to book a trainer or nutritionist, we store your account details and the booking information you choose to share, so that you and your professional can work together.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account at any time: how to delete your account.

PlateLoop is operated by the PlateLoop team, Pakistan. Contact: support.plateloop@gmail.com.

1. Data that stays on your phone

  • Your profile: name, age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goal and daily targets.
  • Your logs: meals and nutrition values, water, activity and steps, sleep, mood and daily reviews.
  • Your reminders and app settings.

This data is not sent to PlateLoop or anyone else, even if you have an account. It is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. It leaves your phone only if you export it yourself.

2. Data we store if you create an account

Accounts are optional; they are needed only to book professionals or to work as one. Account data is stored by our hosting provider, Supabase, on secure servers, and is encrypted in transit.

Data Why
Name, email address, account type, password (stored only as a secure hash by Supabase) To create and secure your account and to contact you about it
Booking requests: the service, appointment time, and the goals or health notes you choose to write So the professional you book can help you
Plans, messages and reviews you exchange with a professional To deliver the service; only the two people in a booking can read its plans and messages
Payment details for manual transfers: amount, transaction ID and the receipt screenshot you upload To confirm your payment. Receipts are stored privately and seen only by you and PlateLoop’s payment team
For professionals: public profile, services, availability, earnings and payout account details To list you in the marketplace and pay you
Reports and blocks you make To keep the community safe; the person you report is not told who reported them

We never collect card numbers. We do not access your contacts, location, camera or microphone. You choose receipt images with the system photo picker; PlateLoop sees only the image you pick.

3. Who can see your data

  • Your professional sees your name, booking request, plan progress and messages once you book them; you see theirs.
  • Everyone can see a professional’s public profile and published reviews. Reviews show only the reviewer’s first name and last initial.
  • PlateLoop’s admin team can see account details, bookings, payment records and reports in order to run the service, verify payments and handle reports. Admins cannot read your chats, except for a message you report, which is copied into the report.
  • Service providers: Supabase (hosting and email delivery through our email provider). They process data only on our behalf.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We may disclose data if the law requires it.

4. Online food search

When a food is not in PlateLoop’s built-in list, the app searches the free Open Food Facts database. Only the food name you typed is sent: no account, device identifier, location or log data. Open Food Facts processes the request under its own privacy policy.

5. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone by Android. Booking and payment updates are shown inside the app when you open it.

6. How long we keep data

  • Phone data: until you delete it in Settings or uninstall the app.
  • Account data: while your account exists.
  • After you ask to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days. If you made or received payments, the payment records (amounts, dates, transaction IDs, receipts and payout records) are kept, without your name or contact details, for as long as accounting and tax law requires.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Export your phone data: Settings → Export data.
  • Delete your phone data: Settings → Delete all data.
  • Delete your account: in the app, Settings → Request account deletion, or by email. See how to delete your account.
  • Correct your profile in the app at any time.
  • Ask what we hold about you, or anything else about privacy: support.plateloop@gmail.com.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your sign-in session is kept in Android’s encrypted storage. Access to account data is limited by database rules so that each person can read only their own data and the bookings they take part in.

9. Health information

PlateLoop provides general wellness estimates (calories, BMI, suggested targets). It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Professionals on PlateLoop are independent and responsible for their own advice. Talk to a doctor before major changes to your diet or exercise.

10. Age

PlateLoop is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
